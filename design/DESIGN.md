## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the architectural, modernist urban identity of Grup Núñez i Navarro’s premier parking network across Barcelona. The personality balances institutional solidity, civic elegance, and seamless digital utility. It evokes trust, clarity, spatial order, and effortless mobility in high-density urban environments.

The visual style blends **Contemporary Architectural Minimalism** with **Precision Mobility Engineering**:
- **Pristine surfaces & subtle atmospheric depth:** Airy, light-flooded viewports built on clinical architectural whites and soft glacial ice tints, counterpointed by authoritative Mediterranean deep petrol blues.
- **Structural clarity:** Precision 1px structural framing, razor-sharp geometric alignment, generous spatial breathing room, and zero ornamental noise.
- **Data transparency:** Functional, technical typography for real-time occupancy, pricing, and electric vehicle metrics, balanced by human, highly legible interface typefaces.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from maritime petrol blue and Barcelona's limestone and modern glass architecture:

- **Primary (`#164250`):** The signature Deep Petrol Blue (Azul Petróleo), representing heritage, reliability, and institutional security. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, primary navigational points, and strong interactive states. Its darker counterpart (`#0F323D`) governs hover states and pressed interactions.
- **Secondary (`#277598`):** Mid Petrol Blue, utilized for secondary accents, active selection lines, focused link targets, and secondary visual indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#E6EFF3`):** Glacial Ice Petrol. Direct homage to NN Parking's clean segmented pills (e.g., *Abonos Mensuales / Reservas*), container badges, active selection backplates, and subtle hero backgrounds.
- **Neutral Palette:** Anchored by pure canvas white (`#FFFFFF`) and pale ice backplates (`#F6F9FA`, `#F8FAFB`). Divider lines and component boundaries use meticulously tuned crisp borders (`#DFEAF0`, `#E2E8F0`, `#C8DFE8`).
- **Semantic / Functional Tokens:** 
  - `success-emerald` (`#10B981`): Real-time EV charging bays, space availability, active passes, and confirmed access.
  - `demand-amber` (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`): Cruising state, limited parking spots, transient demand alerts.
  - `critical-red` (`#D83232` / `#DC2626`): Occupied/full bays, maintenance restrictions, urgent entry limits.

## Typography

The typography pairs Swiss-inspired modernist geometric grotesk clarity with precision engineering data representation:

- **Primary Interface & Editorial Typeface (Inter):** Serves as the primary voice across headlines, body copy, toggles, form fields, and navigation. Headings use Deep Petrol variants (`#1A4B5B`, `#123742`) with tight negative letter-spacing for refined, editorial punch.
- **Metrics & Technical Typeface (JetBrains Mono):** Applied deliberately to quantitative data: live spot counters (e.g. `24 PLAZAS`), hourly tariffs, license plate identification, QR pass codes, EV charge capacity (e.g. `22 kW`), and duration readouts.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display numbers in JetBrains Mono pair with Inter small uppercase labels (`0.02em` tracking) for instantaneous cognitive scanning at parking gates or on mobile booking viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column architectural grid with generous breathing space reflecting airy pavilion designs.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, max container constraint at `1280px` or `1440px` for wide dashboards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 0.75rem`.
- **Rhythm & Alignment:** Strict 4px base spatial unit with 8px increments. Component inner paddings strictly adhere to `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (32px).
- **Split-Pane Architecture:** In booking and real-time mapping flows, a 40/60 split (map canvas sticky on desktop, sliding sheet on mobile) ensures map navigation and parking facility details operate harmoniously.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy, dirty dropshadows in favor of crisp borders, tonal separation, and subtle atmospheric daylight dispersal:

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Canvas background `#F6F9FA` or `#FFFFFF` with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surface / Cards):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards, framed by a 1px border `#DFEAF0` and an ultra-soft tinted ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover Cards / Modals):** White `#FFFFFF` or Pale Glacial `#E6EFF3`, framed with `#C8DFE8` and an elevated petrol-tinted shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Night Petrol Surfaces / Persistent Overlays):** Used for nocturnal HUDs, digital terminal access, and primary mobile bottom bar overlays: surface `#0D2730` or `#0A2129` paired with an inner border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs calibrated rounded geometry that softens structural architectural lines without appearing overly playful:

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px border-radius) creating a refined modern container feel.
- **Pills, Badges & Segmented Switches:** Fully rounded pill-shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for search inputs, occupancy tags, filter toggles, and status badges.
- **Input Fields & Small Controls:** `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) with sharp 1px hairline borders (`#DFEAF0` or `#C8DFE8`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Petrol (`#164250`), text white, `rounded-xl` or `rounded-full` (for primary search CTAs), 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. Transitions to `#0F323D` on hover with a crisp focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary (Glacial):** Background `#E6EFF3`, border `1px solid #C8DFE8`, text `#164250`. On hover, background shifts to `#D8E7EE`.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px solid #DFEAF0`, text `#1A4B5B`.

### Segmented Switch (NN Parkings Signature Toggle)
- Direct adaptation of the *Abonos Mensuales / Reservas* toggle:
  - Outer container: `rounded-full`, background `#E6EFF3`, border `1px solid #C8DFE8`, 4px inner padding.
  - Active pill: Pure white `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-full`, text `#164250`, font-weight `600`, elevated with `0 2px 4px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.08)`.
  - Inactive pill: Transparent, text `#738891`, hover text `#164250`.

### Facility & Metric Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DFEAF0`, radius `rounded-2xl`, padding `1.5rem`.
- Header: Facility name in Inter SemiBold (`#1A4B5B`), accompanied by real-time status pill.
- Data Row: JetBrains Mono spot counter: e.g., Available spots highlighted in `#10B981` (Emerald), or Limited in `#D97706` (Cruising Amber). EV icon in Emerald.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFB`, border `1px solid #DFEAF0`, radius `rounded-xl`.
- Height: 48px standard, 56px for main location discovery inputs.
- Active state: Border transitions to `#164250` with an ambient glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 66, 80, 0.12)`.
- Placeholder text in `#738891`.

### Chips & Occupancy Badges
- **Available / EV Ready:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **High Demand / Cruising:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **Full / Restricted:** Background `rgba(216, 50, 50, 0.1)`, text `#D83232`, border `1px solid rgba(216, 50, 50, 0.2)`.
- All chips utilize `rounded-full`, 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding, with uppercase labels formatted via JetBrains Mono 11px.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18px × 18px. Radios are circular, checkboxes use 4px rounded radius.
- Unchecked: Border `1.5px solid #C8DFE8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Border `#164250`, background `#164250`, inner check or dot in pure white.
